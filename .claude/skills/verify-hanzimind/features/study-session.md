# Study session

A signed-in learner opens `/study` to see every deck on their study list, opens a deck's study settings to pick which of the four study types (reading, listening, understanding, writing) the deck quizzes, and presses `Start studying` to enter `/study/[deckId]`. The session serves one card at a time. A never-seen item arrives as a `New word` intro card that the learner reads and continues past. A seen item arrives as a quiz card for one study type with a single text input, and after `Check` or Enter the result card names the item's new growth stage, offers to accept a wrong meaning as a synonym, and moves on with `Next` until `All done!`.

## Sub-features

- `study-empty` shows the `No decks yet` empty state with a `Browse decks` link when the study list is empty.
- `study-deck-card` lists each saved deck with its grown count, growth bar, status line, and `Start studying` link.
- `study-settings` opens `Update study settings` from a deck card, toggles the four study types, and saves them.
- `study-settings-guard` disables saving when every study type is switched off.
- `study-add-deck` adds a deck to the study list from `/decks` or `/decks/[deckId]` through the same settings dialog.
- `study-new-card` serves a never-seen item as a `New word` intro card and marks it seen on `Continue`.
- `study-reading` shows the characters and grades a pinyin answer.
- `study-listening` plays the audio and grades a pinyin or character answer.
- `study-understanding` shows the characters and grades an English meaning.
- `study-writing` shows the meaning and grades a typed character answer.
- `study-submit` submits with the `Check` button, the Enter key, or `Give up`.
- `study-result` shows `对! Nailed it` or `Not quite` with the new growth stage and the full item.
- `study-synonym` accepts a wrong understanding answer as a synonym from the result card.
- `study-complete` shows `All done!` when nothing is due and returns to `/study`.
- `study-progress` updates the deck card figures on `/study` after answers.
- `study-api-next` returns the next card for a deck over the RPC endpoint.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Choose `Study` in the header nav while signed in. It links to `/study`.
- Open `/study` directly while signed in.
- Choose `Start studying` on a deck card at `/study`. It links to `/study/<deckId>`.
- Open `/study/<deckId>` directly for a deck already on the study list.
- Choose `Save to Study List` on a deck card at `/decks`, or `Save Deck` on `/decks/<deckId>`, to put a deck on the list first.

## Driving it with claude-in-chrome

Preconditions:

- A lane is up on `PORT` and the learner `verify@hanzimind.test` is signed in on the tab, per `README.md`.
- The learner has no study list yet. Before anything below, save the seeded `HSK 1` deck (`deckId` `deck-hsk1`) by `deck-browse-and-save.md`, or create a smaller deck by `deck-create.md` and note its `deckId` from the `/decks/<deckId>` URL. A deck that contains at least one character gives the fullest run, because its components are always included and the character unlocks after them.
- The first card in a fresh deck is a `New word` intro for a component, then the same item comes back as a quiz card. A meaning-only component is quizzed on `Understanding` only. A character with all four types on is quizzed on `Reading` first.

- **Empty list.** Open `/study` with nothing saved. Call `navigate` with `http://localhost:PORT/study`. The heading reads `My study decks`, the card reads `No decks yet` with `Add a deck to your study list and Mika will start growing it with you.`, and a `Browse decks` button links to `/decks`.
- **Add a deck from the browse page.** Open `/decks` and choose the deck's `Save to Study List` button. Call `navigate` with `http://localhost:PORT/decks`, then `find` with `Save to Study List button on the <deckName> card`, then `computer` `left_click` on the ref. A dialog titled `Add Deck to Study List` opens with the description `Choose how you want to study "<deckName>".`, four switch rows (`Reading`, `Listening`, `Understanding`, `Writing`), `Cancel`, and `Add to Study List`. Click `Add to Study List`. A toast reads `Deck added to your study list!` and the card button now reads `Study Settings` with a `Saved` marker. From `/decks/<deckId>` the same dialog opens from the `Save Deck` button in the page header, with the description `Configure your study settings for this deck.` and the same save button.
- **Deck card.** Return to `/study`. Call `navigate` with `http://localhost:PORT/study`, then `get_page_text`. The card shows the deck name, `By @<username>`, the description, `0 of <total> grown`, a growth bar (role `img`, label `<n> of <total> items growing` or `No items yet`), a status line reading `<n> new to plant`, and a `Start studying` button. A deck with reviews waiting reads `<n> ready to review`, a finished one `All caught up`, an empty one `Nothing to study here yet`. A `<n> locked` figure appears when items are still gated behind their parts.
- **Open settings.** Choose the gear on the card. Call `find` with `Study settings for <deckName>` (that string is the button's aria-label), then `computer` `left_click`. A dialog titled `Update study settings` opens with `Choose how this deck quizzes you.` The four rows each carry a label, a hint, and a switch. The switch ids are `readingEnabled`, `listeningEnabled`, `understandingEnabled`, and `writingEnabled`, and the row label doubles as the switch's accessible name, so `find` with `Writing switch` resolves it. Hints read `See the characters, type the pinyin.`, `Hear it spoken, type the pinyin.`, `See the characters, recall the meaning.`, and `Read the meaning, type the characters.`
- **Change a type.** Switch `Writing` off and save. Click the `Writing` switch with `computer` `left_click`, then `find` with `Update settings button` and click it. A toast reads `Deck settings updated!`, the dialog closes, and the card's figures refetch. Read the row back with `select reading_enabled, listening_enabled, understanding_enabled, writing_enabled from user_decks where deck_id = '<deckId>';`, which shows `writing_enabled` false.
- **Settings guard.** Switch all four off. The note under the rows changes from `The parts of each word are always included...` to `Pick at least one mode — otherwise there's nothing to review.` and the `Update settings` button is disabled. Switch one back on before saving, or `Cancel`.
- **Start a session.** Choose `Start studying`. Call `find` with `Start studying link on the <deckName> card`, then `computer` `left_click`. The URL is `/study/<deckId>`. Call `get_page_text`. The first card is the intro card, below.
- **New word card.** The intro card shows a `New word` heading with `Meet it before you're quizzed.`, the full entry from the dictionary component (the glyph, its pinyin where it has one, a `Play audio` button where audio exists, a `Definition` card with the meaning, a `Stroke Order` card for a character, a `Decomposition` card, and an `Entry view` group with `Details` and `Graph` buttons where a graph exists), and a `Continue` button. Audio plays once on mount when the item has any. Take a screenshot with `computer` `screenshot` and `save_to_disk`. Record the pinyin and the `Definition` text, because the next card quizzes this item. Click `Continue` with `computer` `left_click`. There is no result card for an intro. The next card appears. Read back `select seen from user_vocab_items where user_id = '<userId>' and vocab_item_id = '<vocabItemId>';` to see `seen` true.
- **Quiz card layout.** Every quiz card carries a type badge (`Character`, `Word`, `Sentence`, or `Component`) followed by `· Reading`, `· Listening`, `· Understanding`, or `· Writing`, one prompt card, one text input, a `Give up` ghost button, a `Check` submit button, and a hint line. The input has no label and no data-testid, so locate it by placeholder. It is focused on mount.
- **Reading card.** The prompt card shows the characters large. The input placeholder is `Pinyin — e.g. ni3 hao3` and the hint reads `Tones as numbers (hao3 → hǎo) · type v for ü (nv3 → nǚ) · Enter to check`. Call `find` with `pinyin text input`, then `computer` `type` with the numbered form, for example `ren2`. The field converts it to tone marks as you type (`rén`). Tones are required, so `ren` grades wrong.
- **Listening card.** The prompt card is a round button with aria-label `Play audio` and the caption `Tap to play again`. Audio auto-plays once on mount. The input is the same pinyin field as reading. Either the pinyin with tones or the exact characters grade correct. Call `find` with `Play audio button` and click it to replay.
- **Understanding card.** The prompt card shows the characters plus a `Play` button when the item has audio (a meaning-only component has none). The input placeholder is `Enter English meaning` and the hint reads `Press Enter to check`. Call `find` with `English meaning text input`, then `computer` `type` with the definition text you recorded from the intro card.
- **Writing card.** The prompt card shows the English meaning in large accent text. The input placeholder is `Type the characters (e.g. 你好)` with the hint `Press Enter to check`. Call `find` with `characters text input`, then `computer` `type` with the exact characters. Only an exact match grades correct.
- **One answered card.** On the quiz card that follows `Continue`, type the recorded answer and submit. Call `computer` `type` with the answer, then `computer` `key` `Return` (Enter submits from anywhere on the page), or `find` with `Check button` and click. A second submission while the first is in flight is ignored. The result card replaces the quiz card.
- **Result card.** Call `get_page_text`. A correct answer shows the heading `对! Nailed it` with `That one's growing nicely.` and a confetti burst; a wrong one shows `Not quite` with `No stress — it'll come back around soon.` Below the heading, a small card holds the growth tracker, which is a sprout icon, five pips, and the stage label. That stage label is the level after the answer. Level 0 reads `Not started`, 1 `Seedling`, 2 `Sprout`, 3 `Sapling`, 4 `Blooming`, 5 `Evergreen`. A first correct answer therefore reads `Seedling`; a wrong answer resets to `Not started`. The tracker has no aria-label, so assert on the visible label text. Under it, a bordered item card shows the type badge, the pinyin (absent for a meaning-only component), a `Play` button where audio exists, a `Meaning` section with the translation, a `Remember it` section when a memory aid is chosen, and a `Built from` section listing a character's parts. Then a `Next` button and `Press Enter to continue`. Take a screenshot with `computer` `screenshot` and `save_to_disk`. Read back `select reading_level, listening_level, understanding_level, writing_level, seen from user_vocab_items where user_id = '<userId>' and vocab_item_id = '<vocabItemId>';` and confirm the column for the study type answered is `1` after a correct answer.
- **Give up.** On a quiz card, choose `Give up`. Call `find` with `Give up button` and click. It submits an empty answer, which grades wrong, and the result card reads `Not quite` with the stage label `Not started`.
- **Synonym.** On an understanding card, type a wrong but non-empty meaning and submit. The result card adds a button reading `My answer was right — accept "<your answer>"` between the item card and `Next`. It appears only for understanding, only when the answer was wrong, and only when it was non-empty. Call `find` with `accept my answer button` and click. A toast reads `Saved — we'll accept that answer next time.` and the button is replaced by `"<your answer>" will be accepted from now on`. Read back `select synonym from user_vocab_synonyms where user_id = '<userId>' and vocab_item_id = '<vocabItemId>';` to see the answer stored lower-cased and trimmed. The same answer grades correct on that item's next understanding card.
- **Next.** Choose `Next`. Call `find` with `Next button` and click, or `computer` `key` `Return`. The next quiz or intro card appears. Answering or continuing every due card ends the session.
- **Completion.** When nothing is due, the page shows `All done!` with `Great work — you've cleared every card in this session.` and a `Return to study` button. Click it with `computer` `left_click`. The URL is `/study`.
- **Progress after answers.** Back on `/study`, call `get_page_text`. The card's `<n> of <total> grown` count, the growth bar label, the legend under it (stage labels with counts, hidden while every count is zero), and the status line move with the answers just given. The session page itself shows no counter; this card is where progress is read.
- **API next card.** Fetch the next card directly. Run `curl -s -o body.json -w '%{http_code}' -b jar.txt -H 'content-type: application/json' -X POST http://localhost:PORT/api/rpc/study/nextVocabItem -d '{"json":{"deckId":"<deckId>"}}'`. The input is exactly `{ deckId: string }`. HTTP `200` and `body.json` holds a `json` object whose `studyType` is one of `new`, `reading`, `listening`, `understanding`, `writing`, or `null` when nothing is due. A `reading` card carries only `id`, `vocabItem`, `vocabType`, and `studyType`; a `new` card carries the whole item plus `constituents`. The cookie jar is the one written by the sign-in `POST /api/auth/sign-in/email` in `README.md`.
- **Unsaved deck.** Open `/study/<deckId>` for a deck that is not on the study list. Call `navigate` with that URL. The page shows the error boundary card titled `Something went wrong` instead of a card. The same call over `curl` returns a non-200 status.
- **Proof.** Keep the intro-card screenshot, the result-card screenshot, the `get_page_text` output containing the stage label, and the `user_vocab_items` row before and after.

## Gotchas

- Nothing to study is a precondition failure, not a bug. The learner starts with an empty study list, so `/study` is empty and `/study/<deckId>` errors until a deck is saved with at least one study type on.
- Enter is a page-wide key on both the quiz card and the result card. Pressing it once on the result card advances to the next card; pressing it twice answers the next card with an empty string and grades it wrong. Send one `Return` per step and read the page between.
- The `Check`, `Give up`, and `Continue` actions all mutate `user_vocab_items`. Every answer moves a level and a next-review time, so read the row before and after rather than assuming the starting level is zero.
- A correct answer schedules the next review ten minutes out, so a two-item deck can hit `All done!` after one pass. Do not expect the same card twice within a run.
- Reading and listening answers require tone marks or tone numbers. The field converts `ni3` to `nǐ` as you type, and `v` becomes `ü`, so `computer` `type` the numbered form and let the field convert it.
- The input placeholders and several result-card strings contain a real em dash (`Pinyin — e.g. ni3 hao3`, `My answer was right — accept "..."`, `Saved — we'll accept that answer next time.`). Match on the prefix or use `find`, and do not retype the dash by hand.
- The listening card has no visible characters, so the answer has to come from the intro card that introduced the item or from the `Play audio` button. Record the pinyin at the intro card.
- Audio and the correct-answer confetti are not asserted by text. `canPlayAudio` hides the `Play` button on a meaning-only component, so an absent button there is correct.
- The deck settings dialog on `/decks` uses title case (`Update Study Settings`, `Save Settings`, `Add Deck to Study List`, `Add to Study List`) while the one on `/study` uses sentence case (`Update study settings`, `Update settings`). Match the page you are on.
- `/study` shows no `Locked` figure and no legend when the counts are zero. A missing legend on a fresh deck is expected.
- The `Study settings for <deckName>` button is icon-only. Use the aria-label, not visible text.
- The `deckProgress` and `nextVocabItem` queries are cached for 60 seconds and invalidated on answer. Reload `/study` after answering over `curl`, because a `curl` answer does not invalidate the browser's cache.
- The `Entry view` toggle on the intro card only renders when the item has a graph. Its buttons are `Details` and `Graph` with `aria-pressed`; parts are not links on the intro card.
